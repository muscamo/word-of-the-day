Privacy Policy

Word of the Day app · Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Word of the Day is a simple vocabulary app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects (very little) and how it is used.

Data We Collect

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

  • All user data (favorites, history, settings) is stored locally on your device only using AsyncStorage.
  • No account is required to use the app.
  • No data is sent to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The app fetches word definitions from the Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev). When you load a word, a network request is made to their servers containing only the word being looked up — no user identifiers are included.

Please review their privacy policy at dictionaryapi.dev.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app schedules a local push notification on your device. No notification data is transmitted externally.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no advertising, in-app purchases, or social features.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
