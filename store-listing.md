## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Discover a new word every single day and expand your vocabulary effortlessly.

Word of the Day delivers one carefully chosen word each morning — complete with its definition, pronunciation, part of speech, example sentence, and synonyms. It's the simplest way to become a more articulate, confident communicator.

✦ FEATURES

📖 Daily Word — A fresh, interesting word every day. Curated from 400+ words spanning all parts of speech.

★ Favorites — Save the words you love and build your personal vocabulary list.

📅 History — Scroll back through the last 30 days of words. Never miss a day.

⚄ Random Word — Tap for an instant random word whenever you want an extra challenge.

🔔 Daily Reminder — Optional notification at your chosen time to make vocabulary a daily habit.

🌙 Light & Dark Mode — Beautiful in both themes, follows your system setting automatically.

📴 Offline Ready — Once loaded, today's word is cached so it works without internet.

✦ WHY WORD OF THE DAY?

Learning one new word a day is one of the most effective ways to improve your vocabulary, writing, and communication skills. Over a year, that's 365 new words — effortlessly.

Whether you're a student, a writer, a professional, or just someone who loves language, Word of the Day makes vocabulary growth a simple, satisfying daily habit.

✦ COMPLETELY FREE
No ads. No subscriptions. No account required.
